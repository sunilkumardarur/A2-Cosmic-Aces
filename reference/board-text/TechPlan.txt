TECH PLAN
Recommended defaults. You pick the final engine and platform.
ENGINE OPTIONS
Phaser 3 · TypeScript + Vite
RECOMMENDED
Runs in any browser from a link. Fastest to build and share. Can be wrapped for PC (Electron) and phone (Capacitor) later.
Godot 4
Free, light, exports to PC, web and phone from one project. Good if you want app stores.
Unity
Most tools and store support, but heavier than a 2D shooter needs.
PLATFORM & SCREEN
Launch: desktop browser, 16:9, game canvas 1280 × 720 that scales to fit.
Next: phone landscape with touch controls (solo only).
Later: installable PC app and app stores.
Art: SVG shapes in these boards become sprite sheets (PNG) at 2× size.
Stack: TypeScript, Phaser 3, Vite. Tests: Vitest (logic and data), Playwright (screens and play-through). PC app: Electron. Phone: Capacitor.
SAVING
Each profile keeps its own credits, shop items, unlocks, high scores and achievements, plus 3 save slots + 1 autosave per profile (up to 4 profiles per device), in the browser (localStorage). An Export code button copies the save as text for backup or moving devices. Online cloud saves can be added later.

GAME DATA FILES
jets.json · speed, fire rate, damage, hull
pilots.json · Ayan, Aarav, special moves
bosses.json · 40 bosses, HP, phases, attacks
stages.json · 100 stages, bosses, credits
stage_themes.json · enemies, hazards, mini-bosses
waves.json · spawn timing per stage
balance.json · drops, damage, enemy scaling
powerups.json · type, time, drop weight
Tuning numbers lives here, not in code, so you can balance without a programmer.
